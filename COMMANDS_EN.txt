NORTE_STAFF - STAFF COMMANDS
============================

They only work with the whip.devtools permission and developer mode turned on in /w.
Turning developer mode off also turns off noclip, god and invisible.
Every use is written to the server console.
[id] is optional. <value> is required.


TOOLS
-----
/noclip
    Turns free flight on or off. You are invisible, without collision and take no damage.
    W A S D moves, Space goes up, Ctrl goes down, Shift goes fast, Alt goes slow.
    If you are driving a car, the car flies with you.

/god
    Turns no damage mode on or off. Health, armour, hunger and thirst stay full while it is on.

/invis
    Turns invisibility on or off. Other players cannot see you, and you see yourself at half opacity.


VEHICLES
--------
/car <model>
    Spawns a temporary car where you stand, with its key, and puts you in the driver's seat.
    Example: /car adder
    A new /car removes the previous one. The car is also removed when you leave the server.

/dv
    Removes the car you are in, or the nearest car within 6 metres.
    An owned car goes back to its garage. A street or temporary car is removed.

/fix
    Repairs the car you are in: engine, body, fuel tank and dirt.


PLAYERS
-------
/revive [id]
    Revives a downed player. Without an id, revives you.

/heal [id]
    Sets health, armour, hunger and thirst to full. Without an id, heals you.
    If the player is down, they are revived first.

/kick <id> [reason]
    Kicks the player from the server.
    Warning: it does not check ranks, unlike the kick in the /whip panel.

/freeze <id>
    Freezes the player in place. Use it again to unfreeze.


TELEPORTS
---------
/tp <id>
    Takes you to the player.

/bring <id>
    Brings the player to you.

/tpm
    Takes you to the waypoint on the map.

/tpc <x> <y> <z>
    Takes you to the coordinates.
    Example: /tpc 215.0 -810.0 30.7

/voltar
    Takes you back to where you were before your last teleport.


WORLD AND SERVER
----------------
/hora <hour> [minute]
    Sets the server time for everyone.
    Example: /hora 22 30

/clima <type>
    Sets the server weather for everyone.
    Types: CLEAR, EXTRASUNNY, CLOUDS, OVERCAST, RAIN, THUNDER, CLEARING, FOGGY, SMOG, SNOW, BLIZZARD, SNOWLIGHT, XMAS, HALLOWEEN
    Example: /clima RAIN

/anuncio <text>
    Shows a notice to every player for 10 seconds. 200 characters at most.


CONSOLE
-------
These also work in the server console (txAdmin):
/revive, /heal, /kick, /freeze, /hora, /clima and /anuncio.
In the console, /revive and /heal need an id.

Note: the command names stay in Portuguese (/voltar, /hora, /clima, /anuncio) in game.
